Photography, Artist Name and Inspiration

Schahrsad Shahmirzadi presents her photography and art under the 
artist
 name SchirZan. The 
name
 
comes
 
from
 
Persian
 and 
combines
 
two
 
words
:

Shir (
شیر
), 
meaning
 “
lion
”


Zan
 (
زن
), 
meaning
 “
woman
”

Together, “
SchirZan
” conveys “lion-woman” or “lioness” and embodies the courage to follow one’s own path.

For her, “
SchirZan
” also represents a commitment to standing up for disadvantaged people, bringing visibility to their voices, and advocating for justice, equality, and humanity.

Through photography, she can preserve a moment of lived experience for eternity—a thought that moves her anew each time.

The word “photography” derives from Ancient Greek: 
φῶς
, 
phōs
 (genitive 
φωτός
, 
phōtos
), meaning “light,” and 
γράφειν
, 
gráphein
, meaning “to write” or “to draw.” Figuratively, it means “drawing with light.”

Her deliberate use of the traditional German spelling “
Photographie
,” with “
ph
” rather than “f,” reflects this origin. By consistently choosing this spelling, she makes the connection to the medium’s history visible and emphasises her commitment to working consciously and artistically with light.
